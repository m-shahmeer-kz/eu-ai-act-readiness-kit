## Talking to an AI (Art 50(1))

"You are chatting with an AI assistant, not a person." Show it at the start of every conversation.

## Machine-readable marking (Art 50(2))

Mark every generated image, audio, video or text output in a machine-readable, detectable way (e.g. C2PA content credentials or metadata plus a watermark), robust and interoperable as far as technically feasible. Follow the Commission's Code of Practice on marking and labelling when final.
